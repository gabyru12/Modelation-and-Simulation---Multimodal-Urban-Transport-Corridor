# What the SUMO Engine Can Do: A Capability Tour

This set of short essays is a map of the territory, not a manual. Each file answers one question of the form "what can SUMO do about X?" and tries to leave the reader with a wide sense of the options on the shelf, without explaining how any of them is configured. The earlier, in-depth essays on mechanics have been kept in [../detailed_docs/](../detailed_docs/README.md) for the moments when a capability mentioned here needs to be understood properly.

SUMO, the Simulation of Urban MObility, is best thought of as a workshop with three benches. On the first bench you build the physical world: roads, junctions, lanes, rails, sidewalks and waterways, either drawn by hand, generated abstractly, or imported from real maps. On the second you populate it: vehicles of any kind, pedestrians, passengers, containers and whole fleets, with their routes, schedules and personalities. On the third you run it and look inside: record trajectories, count vehicles at virtual sensors, change a signal while the clock is running, or hand control of a vehicle to your own program. What makes the tool remarkable is how far each bench extends. The same engine can simulate a single roundabout at tenth-of-a-second resolution or a metropolitan network with hundreds of thousands of trips in mesoscopic mode, and it can mix cars, buses, trams, bicycles, pedestrians, trains, ships, taxis and delivery vehicles in one run.

The essays follow the natural order of building a scenario. [01-scope-and-scale.md](01-scope-and-scale.md) sets out the kinds of questions SUMO is used for and the scales it works at. [02-road-structures.md](02-road-structures.md) shows the range of networks that can be built. [03-demand-and-routing.md](03-demand-and-routing.md) shows the many ways of creating and routing traffic. [04-vehicles-and-drivers.md](04-vehicles-and-drivers.md) lists the attributes that give a vehicle its body and its personality. [05-modes-and-people.md](05-modes-and-people.md) covers the transport modes and the people who use them. [06-control-and-infrastructure.md](06-control-and-infrastructure.md) covers signals and the other things that manage traffic. [07-live-control-and-integration.md](07-live-control-and-integration.md) covers interaction with running simulations and with other software. [08-measurement-and-output.md](08-measurement-and-output.md) covers what can be observed. [09-specialised-features.md](09-specialised-features.md) gathers environmental, electric, safety and assistance-system features, and [10-tools-and-boundaries.md](10-tools-and-boundaries.md) closes with the supporting toolbox and with an honest account of what SUMO does not do.
